# Code of conduct

The Peer2Paper community is committed to respectful, harassment-free participation for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, discipline, or role.

Be constructive. Distinguish evidence from opinion, state uncertainty honestly, credit other people's work, respect confidentiality, and accept correction. Scientific disagreement is welcome; intimidation, personal attacks, discrimination, and deliberate misrepresentation are not.

Do not publish personal information, participant-level data, restricted research material, private communications, credentials, or security details without authorization.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to [conduct@peer2paper.com](mailto:conduct@peer2paper.com). Maintainers may edit or remove content, restrict participation, and take other proportionate action. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, subject to legal and safety obligations.
