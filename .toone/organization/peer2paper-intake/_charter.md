# Peer2Paper Intake And Provenance Charter

## Mission
Turn submitted papers, data, code, and permissions into a traceable study case with one explicit scientific claim and evidence graph.

## Responsibilities
- Preserve and register supplied files without overwriting originals
- Map papers, datasets, code, claims, variables, and provenance
- Expose ambiguity and missing evidence without guessing
- Freeze validated study-case packages for downstream audit stages
