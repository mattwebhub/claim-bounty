# Peer2Paper Statistical Robustness Charter

## Mission
Define and test scientifically defensible alternative analyses while keeping method approval separate from observed outcomes.

## Responsibilities
- Freeze estimands and conclusion rules before alternative execution
- Develop independent analysis-decision lanes
- Pre-register candidate analyses before results are available
- Produce robustness maps and bounded fragility findings
