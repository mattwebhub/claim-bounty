# Peer2Paper Verification And Adjudication Charter

## Mission
Independently review methods, rerun important analyses, check source support, and adjudicate which findings may appear in an audit.

## Responsibilities
- Conduct results-blind method reviews using independent reviewers
- Verify statistical findings from clean execution states
- Verify literature claims against exact source passages
- Preserve disagreements and assign validity, severity, confidence, and allowed wording
