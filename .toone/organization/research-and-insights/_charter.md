# Research and Insights Charter

## Mission
Provide reliable, evidence-based research and knowledge synthesis that supports decision-making across the organization.

## Responsibilities
- Conduct research in support of organizational questions and initiatives
- Gather information from authorized internal integrations and external sources
- Verify sources and distinguish evidence from inference
- Synthesize findings into clear, decision-useful outputs
- Identify evidence gaps, uncertainty, and matters requiring specialist or human review
