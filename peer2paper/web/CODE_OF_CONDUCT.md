# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience or role.

## Expected behavior

Be constructive, assume good faith, distinguish evidence from opinion, respect confidentiality and accept correction. Do not publish personal information, restricted research material or private communications without authorization.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers. Maintainers may remove content or participation that violates these expectations and will handle reports as confidentially as practical.
